About Us

Two decades of precision glasswork from Pondicherry

Positioned at Pondicherry, India, Sabari Scientific Glass Equipments was established in 1995. Under the guiding light of the Proprietor, Mr. Ramachandran. R, the company has gained immense recognition in the worldwide market.

We own a sound infrastructure in Pondicherry, installed with the latest machines — every piece of equipment is designed and developed under the supervision of industry experts, using approved-standard borosilicate 3.3 tubes.
