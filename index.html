    ABOUT THE COMPANY

    Precision Glassware, Trusted Since 1995

    Sabari Scientific Glass Equipments, an ISO 9001:2015 certified company based in Ariyankuppam, Pondicherry, is a Manufacturer, Exporter and Supplier of laboratory glasswares, laboratory burettes, conical flasks, laboratory funnels, laboratory tools, reagent bottles, weighing bottles and more.

    We use Borosilicate 3.3 tubes and approved-standard designs to ensure quality workmanship, and offer our laboratory equipment under our own registered brand, CORNSIL®. Under the guidance of Proprietor Mr. Ramachandran R., the company has earned recognition across the worldwide market.
